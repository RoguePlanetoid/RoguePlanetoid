RoguePlanetoid Weekly Update #98

26th October 2026

Last week I was at the Microsoft Frontier Tour in London w hich was my first official Microsoft event in a few years and the first since becoming a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional for .NET and Windows Development. It was great to be there to check out the stands along with the keynote and other talks along with a chance to see and try out the Surface Laptop Ultra which is amazing and despite being used all day was still cool to the touch, something I'll have to save up for and get myself if I can! Although I had to leave early to make sure I got back okay it was an interesting and informative day at the event and look forward to the chance to go down there again sometime soon.

This week I will be attending DevHub North in Newcastle upon Tyne where there will be talks about Designing and Building Scalable Applications by Anthony Porthouse and another on AI won't replace great developers - but it will change how they work by Will North. It is always a great event and as usual will be covering it here on this website and it is great to go along to another local event with this month being full of events to go to both in London and Newcastle upon Tyne.

This week I will be presenting at GitHub Copilot Dev Days in Newcastle upon Tyne where there talks from myself on Take Stock of Model Context Protocol with .NET on how GitHub Copilot can work with external tools and data through Model Context Protocol with .NET and another from Godwin Jimmy Akpabio who will look at what happens when AI agents become customers from agent-ready APIs to autonomous payments. There will also be a hands-on GitHub Copilot workshop to put things into practice.
