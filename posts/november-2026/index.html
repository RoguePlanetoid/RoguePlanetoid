Zunepedia Fifth Anniversary

1st November 2026

Zunepedia Fifth Anniversary is this year, which you can read more about in my Article, since it was first published in September 2021, and Zune itself celebrates its twentieth anniversary this month since it was first released in November 2006, so to mark those anniversaries it has been updated with a new front and back cover along with updates to the foreword and epilogue but still keeps the original images except those on the front and back cover. You can get the updated version of Zunepedia at zunepedia.com.

Zunepedia Creator, which you can read more about in my Article, is a brand-new feature where you can generate downloadable images of your own Zune or just have a look at the various model and colour combinations you can create from the original Zune 30 released in 2006, the year later Zune 80 or Zune 120GB, the ultra-portable 4, 8 or 16 GB Zunes along with the premium Zune HD released in 2009. Zunepedia Creator is built using Blazor from Microsoft you can try it out at zunepedia.com/creator.
